## Observation: `matchingSize` semantics differ per command upstream, and it matters for `<sup></sup>`
- **Context**: choosing what `CommandCreoleExposantChange.matchingSize`
  returns.
- **Finding**: upstream returns the INNER capture's length
  (`CommandCreoleExposantChange.java:72-78`), not the full match, and
  `StripeSimple#searchCommand` only tests `!= 0`. So an EMPTY capture
  (`<sup></sup>`) is a NON-match upstream and falls through to literal text.
  `CommandCreoleSizeChange.ts` in this port deliberately diverges (returns
  the full length) to avoid exactly that; for exposant the inner-length
  reading was ported verbatim because the empty-capture fallthrough is real
  jar behavior, and it is covered by a test.
- **Impact**: do not "harmonise" the two commands' `matchingSize` later —
  the difference is upstream's, and one of the two is load-bearing.
- **Confidence**: High (read `CommandCreoleExposantChange.java`,
  `CommandCreoleSizeChange.java`, `StripeSimple.java:305`).
