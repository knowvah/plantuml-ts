## Observation: `render-manifest.ts` only walks `oracle/goldens/**` files literally named `in.puml`
- **Context**: SI30/T0, pinning `test-results/render-manifest-baseline.json` after adding
  `oracle/goldens/{class,description,state}/exposant-0*/input.puml` (the standard
  `input.puml` naming used by the state/class/description DOT-parity suites).
- **Finding**: `scripts/render-manifest.ts#findInPumlFiles` matches only the literal
  filename `in.puml` (`IN_PUML = 'in.puml'`), applied to BOTH `test-results/dot-cache/`
  and `oracle/goldens/`. 484 files under `oracle/goldens/**` happen to be named `in.puml`
  (an older/mixed convention); the other 1425 are named `input.puml` (the convention this
  mission's fixtures and all current DOT-parity suites use) and are silently invisible to
  the manifest — confirmed empirically: `exposant-01-class`/`exposant-02-usecase`/
  `exposant-03-state`'s `oracle/goldens/.../input.puml` copies do not appear in
  `render-manifest-baseline.json` at all; only their `test-results/dot-cache/<type>/<slug>/
  in.puml` mirrors do.
- **Impact**: A future task adding an `oracle/goldens/<type>/<slug>/input.puml`-only fixture
  (no `test-results/dot-cache` mirror) will not be tracked by `render-manifest`/
  `manifest-diff.py` at all — its DOT/SVG changes are invisible to that gate. Always mirror
  a golden into `test-results/dot-cache/<type>/<slug>/in.puml` if it needs manifest coverage.
  Also: `oracle-render.sh`'s `-o <out>` is resolved relative to the **input file's**
  directory, not cwd, when `<out>` is relative — pass an absolute out-dir or the SVG lands
  nested under `<input-dir>/<out>/`.
- **Confidence**: High (read `scripts/render-manifest.ts:47-83`, reproduced empirically).
