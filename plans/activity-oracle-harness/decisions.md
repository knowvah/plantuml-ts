# Architecture decisions — `activity-oracle-harness`

Confirmed 2026-09-02 before decomposition. Treat every one as **locked**.
If a task discovers a conflicting constraint, amend the decision here and
halt for review — do not silently override it.

## D1 — Diff-baseline ratchet, not a byte-freeze golden ratchet

**Context:** 0 of 18 sampled fixtures are zero-diff against the jar; a
freeze gate would gate nothing.
**Decision:** Mirror `tests/oracle/svg-conformance/sequence.diff-baseline.ratchet.test.ts`
— monotone improvement over `oracle/goldens/svg-activity/diff-baseline.json`.
Rise → FAIL naming slug/baseline/new score. Fall → PASS `[IMPROVED]`.
Reaches 0 → PASS `[PROMOTION READY]`, **reported only** — the test never
writes a golden and never copies a file. Create `ratchet.json` empty (as
sequence's is, 0 fixtures) so a promotion path exists later.
**Consequences:** Real regressions are gated from day one; byte-exactness
stays a later goal.

## D2 — The gated quantity is `weightedScore`; `diffCount` is informational

**Context:** `compareSvg` short-circuits on node-type, tag and child-count
mismatch, charging exactly 1 for each however large the skipped subtree —
so `diffCount` is **not monotone in wrongness** (SI34 D5,
`plans/sequence-root-chrome/decisions.md`).
**Decision:** Gate `weightedScore`. Keep `diffCount` as a recorded field.
**Consequences:** This mission WILL trigger the artefact: collapsing the
12-marker defs element converts a short-circuit into real comparison, so
`diffCount` can rise on fixtures whose alignment improved. A risen
`diffCount` beside a fallen `weightedScore` is expected, not a failure.

## D3 — The committed cache is the population of record, not `tests/corpus/`

**Context:** `tests/corpus/` is gitignored, regenerated by
`scripts/populate-corpus.py`, and misclassifies — 79 of its 452 "activity"
files are not activity by our dispatcher.
**Decision:** T0 types every fixture through `registry.resolve` (the
`scripts/oracle-corpus.ts:58` idiom) and captures only
`plugin.type === 'activity'`. `test-results/dot-cache/activity/` is then
the population; nothing downstream reads `tests/corpus/`.
**Consequences:** Reproducible on CI. Re-running `populate-corpus.py`
cannot silently change the gated set.

## D4 — Commit the cache (~2.4 MB measured)

**Context:** Regenerating needs Java, the pinned jar and `~/git/pdiff` —
none present on a CI runner. `.gitignore:25` already re-includes
`test-results/dot-cache/`.
**Decision:** Commit, mirroring sequence. An absent tree means a broken
checkout, not an un-generated one — so **assert presence, never `skipIf`**.
**Consequences:** A gate that cannot silently skip its own input.

## D5 — Pin the pre-chrome baseline BEFORE the chrome fix (ordering)

**Context:** If the baseline is pinned after the fix, the descent is
unmeasurable and unprovable.
**Decision:** T2 pins the 12-diff floor. T5 applies chrome. T6 re-pins,
**naming every fixture whose `weightedScore` rose, with a mechanism**
(SI34 D7 precedent). No silent re-pin.
**Consequences:** Costs one extra commit; buys the only evidence the fix
worked. A re-pin in this repo has adopted a regression before by raising a
pre-existing pin — this is the guard.

## D6 — Chrome scope: route through `document-shell.ts`. No arrowhead port

**Context:** SI34 had to port inline arrowhead shapes for sequence.
**Activity already draws arrowheads as inline polygons**
(`src/diagrams/activity/renderer.ts:44`). The jar emits an EMPTY defs
element; our 12 defs children are `svgRoot`'s auto-injected
`ALL_ARROW_TYPES` markers (`src/core/svg-markers.ts`).
**Decision:** `renderActivity` calls `assembleDocumentShell` with
`data-diagram-type="ACTIVITY"` instead of returning a fragment for the
generic `svgRoot`. `activity-renderer-shapes.ts` is NOT in the write-set.
**Consequences:** Materially smaller than SI34's equivalent task.

## D7 — The two extra `g` children get diagnosed, not guessed

**Context:** Ours has 7 children where the jar has 5 — we emit two EXTRA.
`renderActivity` pushes a full-canvas background rect at
`src/diagrams/activity/renderer.ts:200`; the state renderer's own comment
(`src/diagrams/state/renderer.ts:301-307`) records that the jar draws no
such rect and communicates background via the shell's root `style`
attribute. The second extra is **unidentified**.
**Decision:** T4 states the `~/.claude/rules/diagnosis.md` artifact —
mechanism, origin `file:line`, causal chain, what was ruled out — for
**both** extras before either is removed.
**Consequences:** See the stop condition. If either proves layout-bearing
rather than chrome, T4 halts; it does not license deletion.

## D8 — Parser-gap fixtures record as `status:"error"`, never a number

**Context:** ~90 of the 373 emit a Syntax Error SVG from our parser; the
jar renders 9 of a 10-fixture sample, so these are real gaps in our port.
**Decision:** Capture their oracles, record `status:"error"` with a
reason. Never a numeric baseline.
**Consequences:** A fixture that STOPS erroring is itself a reportable
change (T6), never silently read as "reached 0 diffs". They become a
tracked queue for a later mission.

## D9 — No `parity-activity.json`, no DOT-eligibility gate

**Context:** Verified — zero `svek-*.dot` across 28 jar-rendered activity
fixtures. Upstream `activitydiagram3` never uses dot; its layout is the
ftile/gtile system (`planning/mission-guide.md` G-4).
**Decision:** Omit the AC3-style eligibility check entirely. Follow
sequence, not class/state/object.
**Consequences:** A stray `.dot` for an activity slug is a stop condition,
not a new gate input.

## D10 — Assert that ONLY activity entries move in the render manifest

**Context:** `manifest-diff.py:38` computes moves over `set(base) | set(now)`,
so every added key reads as `unexpected` against the old baseline. The
chrome change alters activity's root for real.
**Decision:** Re-pin in the same task that changes the renderer, and assert
the moved set is exactly the activity slugs. SI34 D6 allowed exactly one
non-sequence entry, named and justified.
**Consequences:** Any unnamed non-activity entry moving is a stop condition.

## Not applicable — backwards compatibility

plantuml-ts has **no consumers**. D6 changes `renderSync`'s output shape for
activity diagrams; that is an improvement toward jar parity, not a breaking
change to manage. No versioning, no deprecation window, no dual-write. The
change is classified only by whether it is revertible: **it is** (one commit
against one file). Record the output-shape change in the commit message; add
a `DIVERGENCES.md` line only if the project's own rules call for one.

## D11 — Pin the new cache tree into the two corpus-completeness gates (added mid-mission, 2026-09-02)

**Context:** Not anticipated at decomposition. `routing-conformance.test.ts`
and `refusal-coverage.test.ts` walk the whole `test-results/dot-cache/**`
tree and FAIL on any fixture absent from their baselines. Adding
`dot-cache/activity/` therefore turns both red — 373 unpinned fixtures each
— and neither baseline is in any task's write-set across all six batches.
T0 hit this as stop condition 1 and correctly refused to write them: the
repo convention (`scripts/repin-sequence-baselines.ts:3-8`) is
"ORCHESTRATOR-ONLY. Task agents never write a baseline JSON."

Both gates' own failure text forbids the cheap fix: *"Measure and pin them —
do not narrow the walk."* Narrowing is how the original 86 misroutes
survived.

**Measured before deciding** (373 fixtures, the gates' own seams —
`fixtureIncludeStore()` + `DeterministicMeasurer`):

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| jar `ACTIVITY`, ours `NONE` | 350 |
| jar error page (`jarErrored`) | 23 — exactly the 23 whose `jarType` is `NONE` |
| `ourType` other than `NONE` | 0 |
| we error AND jar rendered | 82 |

**Decision:** Add task **T0b**, executed by the orchestrator, write-set =
`oracle/goldens/svg-conformance/{routing,refusal}-baseline.json` plus the
derivation-count assertions those two suites carry
(`routing-conformance.test.ts:583`, the refusal sibling's equivalent) and
`.agent-notes/aoh-T0b.md`. Pins are **additive only** — verified by diffing
the JSON and asserting zero pre-existing entries changed.

Routing: 350 `known-misroute` + 23 `jar-error`. All 350 share ONE mechanism,
so this is two judgments, not 373: `renderActivity` returns a
`RenderFragment` with no `diagramType`
(`src/diagrams/activity/renderer.ts:221-226`), so `renderSync` stamps no root
`data-diagram-type`, where upstream stamps it on every exported document
(`TextBlockExporter.java:293`). **That is exactly what T5 fixes** — after T5
all 350 flip `known-misroute` → `agree`, which the gate logs `[FIXED]` and
passes.

Refusal: 373 entries recording today's behaviour, the 82 pinned
`weErrored: true, jarRendered: true, status:"ok"` — the honest-record form,
NOT `known-gap`. This follows the baseline's own stated precedent for
`sequence/nuvoja-46-dezu541` and keeps them inside D8's tracked queue rather
than excusing them.

**Ordering:** T0b commits BEFORE T0. Both gates walk the DISK tree, not the
committed one, and the cache is already on disk — so pinning first leaves
every commit green. Committing the cache first would land one red commit.

**Consequences:** The mission gains a second, independent measurement of
T5's effect (350 `[FIXED]` routing entries) beyond the diff-baseline
descent. Flagged for review: this expands the mission into two prior
missions' gates.

## D12 — A fixture whose GOLDEN is a jar error page is `status:"jar-error"`, not a number (added mid-mission, 2026-09-02)

**Context:** Not anticipated at decomposition. D8 covers fixtures OUR parser
rejects. It says nothing about the opposite case: 23 of the 373 captured
goldens are PlantUML's own graphical error pages, detected by the same needle
both sibling gates use (`PSystemError.java:148-155` /
`ReportLog.java:103-108`). Fifteen of those 23 render CLEANLY on our side, so
a naive baseline would pin a large `weightedScore` comparing a real diagram
against an error image — a number that cannot meaningfully descend and that
would dominate any aggregate.

**Decision:** Record them `status:"jar-error"` with no `weightedScore`,
mirroring `routing-conformance.test.ts` and `refusal-coverage.test.ts`, which
both already exclude exactly this set on exactly this reasoning: *the jar
failing is no evidence about us*. This is existing repo precedent, not a new
category invented for this mission. Detect them from the golden's own content
— never from a slug list, which goes stale.

**Consequences:** The numeric population is 373 − 23 jar-error − 90 our-error
+ 8 counted once (the overlap) = **268 numerically baselined**. The two
exclusion sets overlap by 8; a fixture in both records `jar-error`, since the
jar's failure makes our own outcome unevidential either way.

**CORRECTION, 2026-09-02.** As first written this clause asserted the formula
evaluates to 283 "which is exactly the mission README's own renders clean:
283". That was an orchestrator arithmetic error: `373 − 23 − 90 + 8` is
**268**. T2 measured 268, refused to force the stated total, and flagged the
discrepancy — the correct behaviour. Both numbers are real and answer
different questions: **283** render cleanly on OUR side (373 − 90), and of
those 15 have goldens that are jar error pages, leaving **268** numerically
comparable. Verified: the committed baseline is 268 `baseline` + 82 `error` +
23 `jar-error` = 373, and only `baseline` entries carry a `weightedScore`.

## D13 — The exit bar's `g[1][childCount]` clause is not achievable; amended to the measured reality (2026-09-02)

**Context:** The mission README's exit bar says "`g[1][childCount]` matches
the jar", resting on its premise that ours has exactly 2 extra children on
every fixture (measured pre-mission on two fixtures: 7 v 5, 64 v 62). T4
retired that premise and the orchestrator re-measured it independently with
`compareSvg` itself over all 268 numerically-comparable fixtures:

| `g[1][childCount]` delta (ours − jar) | fixtures |
|---|---|
| ours has FEWER children | **168** |
| already equal (no diff at all) | **20** |
| ours has MORE | **80**, of which **55** are at exactly +2 |

**Decision:** Amend the exit bar. Removing the two background rects fixes the
55 fixtures at +2 and improves 25 more; it CANNOT make the clause true, and
it will give the 20 currently-equal fixtures a −2 childCount diff they do not
have today. The bar becomes: *the 7 root attributes and the defs-childCount
diff are gone from every fixture, and `g[1][childCount]` is fixed on the 55
fixtures whose delta is exactly +2.*

**The 20 currently-equal fixtures are a PREDICTED riser set.** A childCount
mismatch short-circuits the whole subtree and charges
`sumUnits(actual) + sumUnits(expected)` (`compare.ts:398-404`), so acquiring
one can raise `weightedScore` sharply even though alignment improved. T6 must
name them; this decision is their mechanism, stated in advance rather than
reconstructed at re-pin.

**The 168 fixtures where we draw FEWER children than the jar are a fidelity
gap this mission does not touch** — missing ink, not chrome. They are the
single largest item for T6's census and the next mission.

**Consequences:** T6 measures against an achievable bar. Without this
amendment T6 would have failed its own acceptance criteria for a reason no
chrome change could fix.
