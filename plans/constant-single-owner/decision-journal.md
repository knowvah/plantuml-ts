# Decision journal — constant-single-owner

Appended during execution. Every non-trivial judgement call gets a row:
"non-trivial" means a reasonable developer might have chosen differently.

Also record here, because later tasks are measured against them:

- **T1's baseline** — duplicated names, redundant declarations, same-value,
  different-value, known-exceptions.
- **Every classification FLIP** — a row that looked like a `share` and
  turned out to be a `coincidence` on the second read, or the reverse. These
  are the most valuable thing this mission learns: each one is a case where
  equal values were misleading, and their count measures how good that
  heuristic actually was.
- **Every consolidation that was NOT done**, with the reason. A NO is a
  result here, not an omission.
- **Any rendered-output movement, at all** — that is a stop, and the row
  says which constants were involved.
