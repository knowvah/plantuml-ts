**Population.** Every `status: "baseline"` row of `oracle/goldens/svg-activity/diff-baseline.json` with `b0p.json` score ≤ 100 gives **62 rows, Σws 2211**. The brief said 73. The difference is the 11 ws=0 new captures (dajoge … xucero), which are already `status: "pinned"` (add4-b0) and so are not baseline rows. Each row's ws was re-rendered through the ratchet seams (`renderFixtureActivity` + `DeterministicMeasurer` + `compareSvg`), and all 62 matched b0p exactly.

**Method.** Diffs for every row were dumped from those seams, and the mechanisms were read in the Java at `~/git/plantuml/src/main/java/net/`. Each sandbox proof was a temporary edit in a repo copy under `/private/tmp/claude-501/add4-T0c/sb{A,B,C,D}/`; the main checkout was never edited. Every "sandbox drop" is a measured re-render, never an estimate. The proof patches are under `/private/tmp/claude-501/add4-T0c/` (`proofB-*.patch`, the `SBC_*` flags in sbC, and the hacks in sbA/sbD).
